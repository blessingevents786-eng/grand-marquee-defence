About Us

About Grand Marquee Defence

15 Years of Experience

With over 15 years of experience, Grand Marquee Defence has been hosting memorable weddings, receptions, corporate events, and special celebrations.

We specialize in both indoor and outdoor events, offering beautiful settings, elegant décor, and professional arrangements tailored to your occasion.

Good food is an important part of every celebration. We focus on great taste, quality ingredients, and generous portions to ensure your guests enjoy a memorable dining experience.

We also offer live food stations, where selected dishes are freshly prepared and served during the event.

From the venue and décor to delicious food and warm hospitality, our team takes care of every detail to make your event smooth and memorable.
